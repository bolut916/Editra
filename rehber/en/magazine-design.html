Step by step

  1. Open the magazineUse the button below to open Vybble with magazine settings; pick the size and page count. Page one is the cover.
  2. See every page side by sidePages sit next to each other as artboards; Alt-drag a page to copy it or reorder it.
  3. Place the articlesFlow long articles across pages with a text chain. Open each article with a large image and headline.
  4. Try auto designOn a page with an image, ✨ Auto design suggests hundreds of layouts for the same content.
  5. Edit your textTap any headline or text to type; palette and fonts are in the right panel.
  6. PublishCMYK PDF for the printer; a page-flip flipbook or an interactive edition with video and maps for digital readers.

Tips

  • Body text 9–11 pt, 45–75 characters per line; use two or three columns.
  • Don’t repeat the same layout on two consecutive pages.
  • Start page numbers on page two; the cover has none.
  • On the spine side keep text at least 10 mm from the edge.
  • Use at most 3–4 cover lines and keep the masthead in the same place every issue.

Frequently asked questions

What is the standard magazine size?

A4 (210 × 297 mm) and 230 × 300 mm are the most common; 210 × 280 mm is a more economical option.

Can I publish the magazine online?

Yes. Export a page-flip flipbook HTML file and publish or share it.

How many pages should my magazine have?

Saddle-stitched magazines need multiples of 4. School and company magazines are usually 16–32 pages.

Can several people work on one magazine?

For now designs are stored on your device; move a project between devices with a backup file. A team workspace is not available yet.
