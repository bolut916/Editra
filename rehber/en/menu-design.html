Step by step

  1. Open the menu typeUse the button below to open Vybble with menu settings. Pick the size (narrow, A5, A4) and page count, then tap “Start designing →”.
  2. Lay out the sectionsUse separate headlines for starters, mains, desserts and drinks. Five to eight items per section keeps the page readable.
  3. Align the pricesWrite the item name and price on one line with dot leaders, e.g. “Lentil soup ........ 6.50”. The line below becomes the description.
  4. Add photos (optional)Add one or two hero dishes; ✨ Auto design places them without covering the text.
  5. Edit your textTap a headline, text or price to type. Change the palette and fonts in the right panel. 💡 Suggest offers ready-made headings.
  6. Print or share by QRFor print, use Export → PDF. For the table, Export → 📱 QR menu page creates a QR code that opens your menu on guests’ phones.

Tips

  • Keep menu text at least 9 pt; it is often read in dim light.
  • Use no more than two typefaces: a characterful one for headings, a plain one for items.
  • Right-align prices and write the currency the same way on every line.
  • Mark allergens and vegetarian dishes with small symbols and explain them at the bottom.
  • If the menu will be laminated, keep text 5 mm inside the edges.

Frequently asked questions

What size is best for a menu?

A4 for one-page main menus, a 100 × 210 mm narrow menu for cafés and table stands, A5 for dessert and drinks cards.

How should I send my menu to the printer?

As a CMYK PDF with 3 mm bleed and crop marks. Vybble’s Print Centre checks these and your photo resolution.

Can I make a QR menu?

Yes. Export → 📱 QR menu page turns your menu into a mobile page inside the QR link itself; nothing is uploaded to a server.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Vybble is free and needs no account; your designs are stored on your device.
