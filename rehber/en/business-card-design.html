Step by step

  1. Open the business cardUse the button below to open Vybble with business-card settings and pick your printer’s size. Front and back come as two pages.
  2. FrontName and title large, logo small. Put contact details on the back or at the bottom of the front.
  3. BackPhone, email, address and website. Add a QR code if you like (at least 15–20 mm).
  4. Edit your textTap any text to type; palette and fonts are in the right panel.
  5. Send to the printerIn Print Centre set the resolution threshold to 600 dpi, turn on bleed and crop marks, and export a CMYK PDF.

Tips

  • Keep text at least 4–5 mm inside the trim line.
  • The smallest text should not be under 7 pt.
  • Avoid thin typefaces on dark backgrounds; they can disappear in print.
  • Check your phone number and email twice before printing.

Frequently asked questions

What size is a business card in mm?

85 × 55 mm is the European standard, 90 × 50 mm is common in Turkey and 85 × 54 mm is credit-card size.

What dpi should a business card be?

600 dpi keeps fine text and logos sharp; at least 300 dpi if it contains photos.

Do I need bleed?

Yes, if a background colour or image reaches the edge: add 3 mm bleed on each side.
