How to make a catalog

Build a product catalog step by step: pick the right size, place products on a grid, add prices and descriptions, then export a print-ready PDF or a page-flip digital catalog.

Step by step

  1. Open the catalogUse the button below to open Vybble with catalog settings. Choose size and page count; page one becomes the cover.
  2. Upload product photosFour to six products per page suits most catalogs. Shoot them in the same light and on the same background for a consistent look.
  3. Automatic layout✨ Auto design places your photos and text on the grid without overlaps. Don’t like it? Tap “🎲 Another”.
  4. Many products? Use CSVWrite placeholders such as {Name} and {Price} on a page and load your product list as a CSV file (⋯ menu → Catalog from CSV). Vybble copies the page for every row.
  5. Edit your textTap any headline, text or price to type; change palette and fonts in the right panel.
  6. Print or shareExport → PDF for print; for customers, send a page-flip magazine or PNG from Export → 📤 Send.

Tips

  • Use the same grid on every page so readers can compare products.
  • Keep price, name and short description in the same order for every product.
  • Saddle-stitched catalogs need a page count divisible by 4: 8, 12, 16…
  • Product photos should be at least 300 dpi at print size; Print Centre flags low-resolution images.
  • Use one strong image and a short title on the cover.

Frequently asked questions

How many pages should a catalog have?

Saddle-stitched catalogs need multiples of 4 (8, 12, 16). 8–12 pages is enough for most small businesses.

What is the best paper size for a catalog?

A4 portrait (210 × 297 mm) is the most common. Use A4 landscape to show products wide, A5 for pocket catalogs.

Do I have to place hundreds of products one by one?

No. Load your product list as CSV and generate the pages with placeholders.

Can I share the catalog digitally?

Yes. Export a page-flip HTML flipbook or PNG and send it via WhatsApp, email or your website.
