How to make a magazine cover

Design an eye-catching magazine, book or album cover: one strong photo, a readable title and the right size. In your browser, for free.

Step by step

  1. Open the coverUse the button below to open Vybble with cover settings and pick the cover type.
  2. Choose one strong photoA cover needs a single focal point. A calm (low-detail) area of the photo should leave room for the title.
  3. Automatic cover layout✨ Auto design puts the title on the calmest area of the photo; if contrast is too low, it places the title on a colour card.
  4. Masthead and cover linesKeep the magazine name at the top and group two to four short cover lines at the side or bottom.
  5. Edit your textTap any text to type; palette and fonts are in the right panel.
  6. ExportCMYK PDF with bleed for print, PNG for social media.

Tips

  • Make sure the title contrasts strongly with the photo: dark text on light photos, light text on dark ones.
  • In portraits the eyes usually sit in the top third of the page.
  • If the photo runs to the edge it must extend into the 3 mm bleed.
  • A full-page cover photo needs at least 300 dpi (about 2717 × 3543 px for 230 × 300 mm).

Frequently asked questions

What size is a magazine cover?

The most common sizes are 230 × 300 mm and A4 (210 × 297 mm). Add 3 mm bleed on each side for print.

How many pixels should a cover photo be?

For 300 dpi on a 230 × 300 mm cover you need about 2717 × 3543 pixels. Print Centre tells you if your photo is enough.

Can I make a book cover?

Yes. The “Book (open)” size puts front and back cover on one page (420 × 297 mm).
