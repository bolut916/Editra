Step by step

  1. Open the posterUse the button below to open Vybble with poster settings and pick the size.
  2. Cut the message to threeA poster needs three things: what (headline), when and where. Make the rest small or move it to a QR code.
  3. Giant headlineMake the headline the largest element; ✨ Auto design tries giant-headline and cover layouts.
  4. Add a QR codeAdd a QR code for tickets or sign-up; make it at least 20 × 20 mm.
  5. Edit your textTap any text to type; palette and fonts are in the right panel.
  6. PrintExport a CMYK PDF. For large posters 150 dpi is usually enough; set the threshold in Print Centre.

Tips

  • The headline should be readable from 5–10 metres: at least 60–80 pt on an A3 poster.
  • Use at most two typefaces and one accent colour.
  • Always keep date and venue in the same corner.
  • 150 dpi is enough for large posters; prefer 300 dpi for A3 posters seen up close.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most common poster size?

A3 (297 × 420 mm) for indoor notices, A2 and 50 × 70 cm for outdoor and events, A0 for large boards.

What dpi should a poster be?

300 dpi for A3 posters seen up close; 150 dpi for large posters seen from a distance.

Can I use the poster on social media too?

Yes. Export it as PNG, or open a new page in a social-media size and copy the design.
